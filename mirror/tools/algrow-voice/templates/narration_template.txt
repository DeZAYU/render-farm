On the night of October twelfth, nineteen ninety-four, a white van stopped at the edge of Lake Hollow.

The driver was never seen again. Police found the engine still running, and the headlights pointed at the water.

Brendan could at least see the headlights coming. The next couple spent their evening listening to the trees.
