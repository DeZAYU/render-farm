Lost Games case 2 v1 (≈20 distinct pictures in 3 min, same farmer frame held ~40 s, repeated silhouette explainer, dark oblique pages) was rejected: "case is so bad… references 50x better… not heavy production".

**Why:** the owner judges against Farrell McGuire/LEMMiNO density. Plain gameplay over VO plus a few devices reads as low effort (same complaint they made about the first case 1).

**How to apply:**
- Give every clause its own picture and every spoken noun a literal crop of the real artifact (macro, rings, line crops, splits). Words go in the shot.
- No single image held more than ~3–4 s unless the camera is moving through a document.
- Pages must be bright, sharp and readable.
- Before building a whole case, prototype the densest stretch and get a yes. See [[user-channel-404-thread]], [[project-lost-games-part2]].

Motion follow-up (same day): a wall of crops drifting via CSS translate plus RGB-fringe word pops was called "so fake and unsmooth". Put multi-element shots on the Space camera rig (eased camera moves between real planes). Words get plain fades, not pops. The rest of the dense prototype (macros, rings, mirror split, question stack, line crops) was approved: "rest looks good".
