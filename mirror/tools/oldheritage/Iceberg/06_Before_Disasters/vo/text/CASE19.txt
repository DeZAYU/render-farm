Skye runs a fishing channel called The Skye Life, and in spring 2022 he went to fish the Oswego River, north of Syracuse, during the shad run. With baitfish pouring through, the big fish were stacked up right under the dam's gates.

Most people fish there from the bank, but Skye waded out about a football field along a line of slippery rocks. A sign warned that the water rises rapidly right after a horn sounds three times. He hadn't seen rain all week, so he figured it wouldn't go off.

For a while the fishing's great, with bass on the line and gar cruising past his feet. Then a horn blasts across the water.

The water didn't rise at all. The only damage was a leg he banged up on the run. Back on the bank, he decided they'd false alarmed him, maybe because somebody spotted him out there and wanted him gone. Below a dam like this, he said, the river can come up three feet in seconds.
