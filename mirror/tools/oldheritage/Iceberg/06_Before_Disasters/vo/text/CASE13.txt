This dashcam was following traffic on a narrow road reported to be the Vladimir bypass, in central Russia, at 5:52 in the evening on September 19, 2012. Overtaking was reportedly banned on this stretch, and there's a container truck just ahead.

Pay attention to the oncoming lane.

The truck ahead had swerved left, straight into a truck coming the other way, and reports said its driver had probably spotted a parked truck too late. That oncoming truck's driver went out through his own windscreen and landed on his feet. He was never named. Seconds later he's walking around his crushed cab, apparently unhurt.
