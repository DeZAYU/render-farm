Two women in Tehran were filming themselves in the front of a car one night in May 2014, singing along to a pop song called "Ghorboni". You never see the road. Reports said the driver let go of the wheel to make a heart with her hands, and the heart comes up about five seconds in.

About fourteen seconds in, there's a bright flash and the picture whites out. The car had crashed head-on.

They posted the clip anyway, and within days it was everywhere. Nobody's independently confirmed it's genuine, but the police treated it as real.

On May 18, the head of accidents at Tehran's traffic police said the crash had never been reported to them. Even so, he said both women had come out of it unhurt, and that they'd broken the law and would be dealt with. Other reports said they were treated in the hospital for minor injuries.
