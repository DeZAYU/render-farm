Picture walking along the seabed at Xcaret Park, near Playa del Carmen in Mexico, in a big helmet fed with air from the surface. It's open at the bottom, so it only holds air while it stays upright.

A woman's filming herself on a selfie stick here, with another walker right behind her at the rail.

Once that walker tipped upside down, the helmet slid off and her air was gone. Park divers reached her and hauled her up.

The clip went viral in June 2025. A woman who said she was in the group wrote that a stingray brushed the tourist's legs, and that the tourist was fine. There's been no public statement from Xcaret. Honestly, one stranger's post is all we've got, and I'd want more than that before calling it a happy ending.
