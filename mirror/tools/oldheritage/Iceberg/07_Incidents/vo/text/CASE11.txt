Bryce Bigelow's helmet camera spends almost three minutes in fresh powder at Brighton Resort in Utah, weaving through heavy pines behind a friend. It's January 15, 2023, and I could watch that run on loop.

Then he lines up on a drop under the Snake lift and jokes that he's going to jump off it and get wrecked. The lip is way bigger than it looked. In the air he panicked and tucked, then started to turn.

He comes down nose first.

His left ski tip dug in, and the binding, the one part built to let go at exactly that moment, didn't release. His body kept going forward over a boot that couldn't move, and his leg snapped.

Someone on the chairlift called it in. He told patrol his buddy was blocking the jump so nobody else came off it. Who thinks about that with a broken leg? Bryce, apparently. Patrol splinted his leg, rolled him into a sled and towed him down the mountain. Two months later, he was back on skis.
