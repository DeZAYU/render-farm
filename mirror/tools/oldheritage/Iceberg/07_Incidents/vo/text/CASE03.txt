The Stawamus Chief is a granite dome that rises more than 2,000 feet over Howe Sound in Squamish, British Columbia. In July 2022, Casey Dubois was on the fourth pitch of a route called Warriors of the Wasteland. A pitch is one rope length of climbing, and this one's a thin finger crack about 100 feet long.

He's placed small spring loaded cams in the crack, each clipped to his rope with a carabiner, a metal loop with a spring gate. Kyle Smith's filming from above, and Casey's got one last reach to go.

He comes off.

Watch the replay. The tiny cam at his ankles rips out, and somehow the carabiner on the piece below comes off the rope. The best guess is it'd flipped as the rope went slack, though Kyle said they couldn't work out how. A carabiner's got one job, and that's staying shut, and I think a loose rock would scare me less.

Casey slid more than half the pitch before the rope caught him. His helmet cracked in several places, but Kyle said he was a little shaken and totally okay.
