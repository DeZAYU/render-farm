A dive light cuts a white tunnel through black water, and the camera on this diver's head sees nothing else. It's a lobster night off Southern California, and he's out with his father at his honeyhole, slang for a place that always pays off.

Divers here can only take spiny lobsters by hand. A scared one backs deep into the rock and braces against the walls, so you follow it in. Reaching into a black hole underwater for dinner? I'd pass, and that's before anything goes wrong.

The yellow mesh bag keeps filling. Then he chases a big one further into a cave, and the rock closes in around him. He wins the grab, and somewhere in here, by his own account, he loses the way out.

He finds it again, and his father's light shows up in the water. Between them they took two limits, about 14 lobsters, plus a six-pound cabezon. When he posted the dive in December 2013, he wrote that he nearly drowned down there, and thankfully got out fine.
