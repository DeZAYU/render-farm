A red quad is bouncing down a sandy, puddled trail through pines and palmetto scrub, with a camera strapped to the rider's helmet. The rider is Greg Harris, and he says he rides these trails regularly. For about 25 seconds, nothing happens. Then he swings around a bend at speed.

Don't blink, because it's over in two seconds.

That jerk up into the treetops is a metal wire. Someone had tied it between two trees across the trail, and it caught him under the chin of his helmet, then snapped. He rolled into a clearing and stopped, the wire still stuck under his chin guard and his foot jammed under the shifter. For half a minute, all he could say was "oh my god."

He posted the clip on February 14, 2018, with a short warning to be careful, and he never said who he thought had strung it. After a ride like that, I wouldn't go near that trail again.
