Graham and Ellie travel Britain in a motorhome they call Merlin, and film it all for their channel, Wizard in the Wild. One night in November 2022, a little after one in the morning, Graham stepped out into pouring rain with the camera running to see what had just hit them.

Here's what he found.

They'd been rushing home from the Isle of Arran because of a family emergency, and after a long day of driving they picked a well-reviewed parking lot on Henshall Lane, near Altrincham. They were in bed by eleven. Around one, a car stereo thumped somewhere close and went quiet, and then came one enormous bang. Graham thought a branch had hit the roof, but he'd parked away from the trees on purpose.

Later they found a third brick. Three hits at once meant at least three people throwing, they figured, maybe four with a driver, and nobody ever saw a face. So why them, and not the other motorhome on the same lot? I don't think anyone will ever know. The people inside it had heard a car screech away, and they came over in the rain with garbage bags and tape for the broken window. Graham and Ellie didn't spot the dent in the side until they were home, 200 miles later.
