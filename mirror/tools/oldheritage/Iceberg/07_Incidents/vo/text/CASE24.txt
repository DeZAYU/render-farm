Brendan, the man behind Brendan Explores, has spent hundreds of nights stealth camping, which means sleeping in his van somewhere that isn't a campsite and hoping nobody notices. Early in 2024 he cooked dinner in Jaywick, in Essex, England. When cars and motorbikes started ripping around the streets after sunset, he left town and found a dark, quiet lane somewhere between Jaywick and Colchester, with no houses and nobody around.

Four hours after he'd gone to sleep, at 2:30 in the morning, he was wide awake and filming himself in the dark. He thought someone was outside the van. A flashlight flickered past the curtains, and from here the picture is almost black, so his whispering does the work.

As soon as he shouted, whoever it was ran off up the lane, and a car sped away in the distance. He was unhurt, but his front passenger window was gone.

He swept the glass off his seat and left right then, and I don't blame him. It's a three and a half hour drive home to Shrewsbury, and he didn't stop until he got there, freezing in the cab, just before sunrise. In hundreds of nights out, he said, nothing like that had ever happened to him. His best guess is they'd seen the covered fridge on his front seat and taken it for a toolbox.
