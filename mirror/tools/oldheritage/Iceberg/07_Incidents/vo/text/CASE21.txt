On April 2, 2021, urban explorer Jared Coker squeezed into the old TreatCo plant in Wichita, a pet-treat factory gutted by fires. For almost two hours it's an ordinary video of dark production floors.

From an upstairs window, he saw a red car roll through the gate. A man got out and walked the fence line, and Jared didn't want to be seen, so he started looking for a way out.

Through the windows he caught the man again, carrying something long. Then a loud blast echoed across the site, and Jared thought it was a gunshot. At dusk, he climbed out a window and headed for the trees.

He almost made it.

The man stepped out of the bushes with a gun and made Jared put handcuffs on himself. With the camera rolling, he said the "use of deadly force" painted on the wall wasn't graffiti.

Police took about an hour and a half to arrive, he says, and Jared was taken in for criminal trespass and paid a 200 dollar fine. That blast earlier wasn't a gun, Jared later said. It'd been a noise device the owner had rigged. I'd call Jared lucky, and what happened next shows just how lucky.

About 15 minutes after Jared was driven off, by his account, two other trespassers came in. None of it's on camera. The plant's owner, 62-year-old Ken Thomas, fired a shotgun at them. Both were hospitalized, and neither injury turned out to be life-threatening.

In July 2023, a Sedgwick County jury convicted Thomas of two counts of aggravated battery, and that September a judge gave him two years' probation.
