### 02 · Weston-super-Mare: Fire Destroys the Grand Pier's Amusement Pavilion
- **Date & location:** Shortly before 7 am, Monday 28 Jul 2008. Grand Pier, Weston-super-Mare, Somerset, England.
- **Opening visual:** A long, steady shot from the shore of black smoke towering over the pier, with flames burning along the pavilion at the end of it.
- **What happened (research):** Fire broke out at the foot of a tower at the pavilion end of the Grand Pier, the pier's covered amusement hall. The pavilion had been revamped that April with a go-kart track, a 40 ft climbing wall, a water attraction and improved fairground rides. A privately monitored alarm went off at 01:35, but the monitoring company could not reach the key-holder and no action was taken; the fire service was not called until 06:46. About 40 firefighters fought the blaze, and the pavilion and its tower were destroyed. Nobody was hurt. A worker, Robert Tinker, carried gas canisters out of the building, which the fire brigade said stopped the fire getting worse. Deep-fat fryers and arson were ruled out; in October 2008 the brigade recorded the cause as unknown, most likely electrical. A new pavilion opened on 23 Oct 2010 (Wikipedia; Attractions Management; Express & Star).
- **Story beats:** alarm goes off in the night → nobody comes → fire found at dawn → 40 firefighters → the pavilion and its rides burn down → nobody hurt → worker pulls out gas canisters → cause most likely electrical → rebuilt in 2010.

### 03 · Busch Gardens Tampa: 16 Riders Stranded on Cheetah Hunt in the Rain
- **Date & location:** Afternoon of Wednesday 12 Feb 2014; the last riders were down just after 6 pm. Cheetah Hunt, Busch Gardens Tampa Bay, Tampa, Florida.
- **Opening visual:** A fire-department ladder bucket raised beside the stalled train on the green track, riders still in their seats.
- **What happened (research):** Cheetah Hunt, the park's longest thrill ride, stalled at a crest in the track shortly after leaving the station; CoasterBuzz reported that staff first tried to push the train back to the platform, without success. Busch Gardens spokesman Travis Claytor said only that "the ride malfunctioned near the loading station." Riders sat in the rain in ponchos for more than three hours, according to CNN. Tampa Fire Rescue brought two ladder companies and used an on-scene cherry picker to take each passenger off; Busch Gardens used its own bucket truck. The park said 15 people were aboard and Tampa Fire Rescue said it removed 16. No injuries were reported. The ride stayed closed the next day while the park worked out why it stopped (CNN; NBC Miami; CoasterBuzz; Tampa Bay Times; Tampa Fire Rescue).
- **Story beats:** winter afternoon at the park → the launched coaster stalls on a crest just out of the station → staff try to push it back, and it won't move → riders sit in the rain for hours → two fire ladder trucks and a cherry picker → riders lifted off one at a time → all down just after 6 pm, nobody hurt → ride closed while the park investigates.

### 04 · Eastbourne Pier: The Arcade Burns as Crowds Watch From the Beach
- **Date & location:** Wednesday afternoon, 30 Jul 2014. Eastbourne Pier, East Sussex, England.
- **Opening visual:** A beachgoer's phone shot of orange flames and black smoke pouring out of the arcade halfway along the pier, with crowds lined up on the shingle in front.
- **What happened (research):** Fire broke out in the arcade building on the Victorian pier during a busy summer afternoon. Police said the pier was "safely and quickly evacuated" and no one was trapped; no injuries were reported. East Sussex Fire and Rescue Service believed the fire started behind some wood panelling, and police said it was not believed to be suspicious at the time. The arcade was badly damaged and the wooden structure around it was severely damaged (Attractions Management; Fox News/AP).
