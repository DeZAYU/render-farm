Owner 4 Nov 2026, on WP01's "PourClock" (the clip framed small beside a red vertical time bar "THE FILMER'S CLIP 0:00–2:30, 1:01 FRAME
BUCKLING"): "new templates were there but it did not stand towards niche".

**Why:** the channel is caught-on-camera footage first (reference: Occult Inquisitor). A UI widget next to a shrunken clip reads as an
explainer/infographic, not a horror-iceberg video, and it shrinks the footage the viewer came for.

**How to apply:** new per-case devices must work ON the footage and keep it big: slow replay + push into the detail, freeze then
punch-in on the moment, a second angle synced side by side, before/after on the real place, a spotlight/vignette on what to watch, the
real document typed sharp over the footage. Never: time bars, clocks, gauges, HUD panels, charts or data dashboards beside a small clip;
never the same layout for most of the case. Footage ≈ 80 % of the picture.
