Owner, 2 Nov 2026, on TP17 (1:09): "raw is too less in between tbh u dont have to add 6 diff times raw but like people wants to know what happenes the incident… they dont wanna watch just 3second its not entertaining at all".

**Why:** viewers come to see the incident; 3 s flashes feel like teasing.
**How to apply:** fewer but longer raws. The main incident raw plays the event through (10–25 s, VO paused or ducked); secondary raws 6–12 s; no 3 s chops. Short footage → slow replay or second angle. In CASE_BUILD_GUIDE.md. Related: [[feedback-iceberg-raw-audio-clean]].
