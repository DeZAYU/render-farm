The owner judges renders against Nocturn/Farrell McGuire and rejects "normal compilation" quality. After seeing case #1 (2026-10-13) they flagged: weak VO→raw hand-over, no channel/source intro beat before the first raw, a red waveform covering the footage, grade/dimming that visibly kicks in mid-shot, white location text blending — and said "I just told you 30% of what is bad".

**Why:** passing our own checklists (contrast, gap timings, LUFS) was not enough; the owner compares against the references by eye.

**How to apply:** before showing any render, run an independent critic pass that compares it side-by-side with reference hand-overs/openings/text/grade (frame strips + measurements) and fix everything found; never rely on builders' self-QC alone. Related: [[wm-pol50-project]]
