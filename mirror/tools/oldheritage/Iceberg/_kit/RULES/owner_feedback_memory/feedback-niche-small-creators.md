The owner (2026-10-27): "no big channels, its scary videos not encounters niche + small creators like they had one incident and now dont js milk like a influencer".

**Why:** the Warrior Mind scary videos are real one-off paranormal moments from ordinary people. Ghost-hunting, urbex and camping-encounter channels farm the niche, and big channels bring rights risk.

**How to apply:**
- Reject camping, hiking, van and urbex "encounters", and stranger or confrontation clips.
- Reject ghost-hunter, investigator and urbex channels, and any creator who posts paranormal content regularly.
- Reject channels with large followings (roughly >20k subs, or a big views pattern).
- Keep home CCTV, baby monitors, doorbells, workplace cams, and personal phone clips from people with one incident.
- Check `channel_follower_count` and the upload history in info.json.

Related: [[case-source-picks]], [[feedback-exclusion-list]].

**Also (2026-10-28):** replace any case with a rights flag ("© All Rights Reserved" in the title or description, licensing credits). Don't keep it as a "caution".
