# OWNER RULES: binding on every case of every video (404 thread channel, OUTLINES5)

Collected from every correction the owner has made. A case that breaks one of these is not finished.

## Story and sound
1. The narration runs from 0:00 of every case. The story order is the script's order: never reorder or drop lines.
2. Credits and names are correct: a person, place or source name only appears over that person, place or source.
3. Footage is shown only where the sentence describes what the footage shows. The clip moment must match the words.
4. Never show fake material: no invented posts, quotes, profiles or messages, and no song titles or fragments presented as quotes.
5. Never show private info: emails, private phone numbers, usernames of private people. Official police tip-lines are allowed.

## Repetition (the owner's biggest complaint)
6. Nothing generic or 1:1 templated. Reference channels never repeat the same template; neither do we.
7. No device twice in a row, except plain footage. No non-footage template more than 3 times in any 2 minutes.
8. Calendar diagram: at most 1 per case, and at most 1 per 3 cases. Other dates rotate through the date family (VCR date, earlier card, date plate, unblur date, year-in-scene).
9. VV icon explainers are fine, but each one must be different and detailed:
   - Vary the form (pair, split, equation, chain, count, compare, timeline), the glyphs, the layout, the colour accent, the camera move and the backdrop.
   - Never the same spec twice in a video.
   - Each one must explain the sentence, not decorate it.
10. Screenshot (Farrell SS) styles rotate: farrellSS, wide macro, crop chain, invert grow, I-beam select, page tilt, page spread. Never the same style twice in a row.
11. Never the same clip moment twice in a case. Two consecutive shots from one clip must be clearly different moments (at least 8 s apart in the source).
12. A photo or page appears at most twice per case.

## Craft
13. Cuts land on the narration beats, with smooth eased motion and one grade per video. No hard jumps in colour or zoom.
14. No news anchors, reporter stand-ups, promos, channel intros, watermarks or news chrome. Crop to the speaker or the event.
15. Never show Wikipedia footnotes, "See also" sections or reference lists. Farrell SS shows only the line that proves the claim.
16. fern 3D only where nothing else can show the situation. No decorative Blender sets. AE-style 3D camera is preferred.
17. Never on screen: typed narration, file names, face mosaics, mixed colour tints, or the QA-rejected templates.
18. Material never carries across cases.

## Process
19. Pilot first. Self-review frames against these rules before showing anything.
20. Phone previews ≤30 MB. Ask before any upload or publish.
21. Clear caches regularly (D:/rtmp/remotion-*, old bundles).
