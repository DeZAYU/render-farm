# Horrors on <Platform>
match: horrors on, internet archive, wayback machine, liveleak, internet explorer, 1990s internet, website, platform, lost media, archive, iceberg

## What the format is
The platform IS the topic. Real platform logo (official file) + one real, raw disturbing photo/footage frame,
VHS grime over everything. Single image, no headline text beyond the logo. Reference variants: the platform's own
logo corrupted into a face (Internet Explorer ref), or logo across the top over a found photo (IA/LiveLeak refs).

## Approved recipe (internet-archive v018, refined in v020)
1. Logo: official Wayback Machine / platform logo → `cli brandlogo` (plate knocked out, greys → off-white, brand red
   kept, bloom). Top third, 60–85% of frame width; small tracked wordmark ("INTERNET ARCHIVE") above it.
2. Black fade from the top down to ~0.55 height under the logo, so the photo has no top seam.
3. Photo: real vintage/found photo (approved: three masked figures, B&W) filling the lower ~60%; subjects centred
   as a group, evenly spaced, faces fully visible, nobody cut by the frame.
4. Integrate: brand-red light spill from the logo onto the nearest subject; subjects may overlap the logo
   (v020: horns cut out and placed in front of "Wayback").
5. Grade: high-contrast B&W/desaturated photo, chromatic split, scanline jitter, colour grain, crunch, vignette.
   Keep detail in whites (masks clipped in v018 — check highlights).

## Mistakes to avoid (cost 2 corrections + ~13 versions)
- Substituting another brand's mascot (Bonzi Buddy, Clippy) because "copying the reference concept isn't allowed" —
  wrong reading. Copy the style; use THIS platform's logo.
- Cartoon/illustrated subjects, logos rebuilt from rectangles with dot eyes.
- Copying the duration badge from the reference screenshot.
- Logo as a clean sticker floating on the photo; bright stray strip at the frame edge.
