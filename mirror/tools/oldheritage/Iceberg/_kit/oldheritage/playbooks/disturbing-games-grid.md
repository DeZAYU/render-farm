# Disturbing Games Grid (2×2 + centre platform logo)
match: games, flash games, ps1, ps2, playstation, console, nintendo, xbox, browser games, most disturbing games, video games, game list, grid

## What the format is
Four real frames from four different games on the list, in a 2×2 grid, with the platform's logo dead centre.
Reference: PS1 grid (Fatal Frame girl TL, creature TR, twin-babies figure BL, bleeding-eyes girl BR, PS logo centre).
His brief slots: TL = hot-but-disturbing girl (full face), one image "to balance out", two plainly disturbing.

## Approved recipe (flash-games v024)
1. Pick 4 games from the video's chapter list; one image per game. For each slot, search the game on Google Images,
   scroll, and show him 5–10 candidates as picture cards; he picks.
2. Mix shot types: e.g. TL face close-up, TR medium shot (doll in doorway), BL full scene (prisoner + beast),
   BR face. Never 4 faces.
3. Each panel: one clear subject, nothing cluttered; uneasy-at-a-glance beats gore.
4. Centre logo: official flat 2D logo (Flash = red rounded square + white "f"), ~15% of frame height × width,
   with a black fade/shadow behind it so it sits in the dark ("disturbing CC").
5. Grade in two steps: (a) measure each panel's mean luma and equalise per panel (before equalisation the panels
   spread 0.13–0.29 → bring them into a tight range); (b) one overall CC (black point, local contrast, light crunch, grain) to unify mixed art styles.
   Result must be visible on a phone but not washed out.
6. Export named as he asks (e.g. "Flash games.png/.jpg") and copy to ~/Downloads.

## Mistakes to avoid (cost ~11 corrections over 24 versions)
- 3D/glossy logo when the brand's real logo is flat.
- Face cropped above the nose; four faces in a row.
- Busy panels (branches + poem text) instead of one disturbing subject.
- Picking images yourself or offering "random selections" instead of real per-game candidates.
- Brightness swings: global boost washes panels out; global darken makes it dull. Equalise per panel.
