# F1 Iceberg Split (disturbing image left + 3-line text right)
match: not human, used to be a human, camcorder moments, dashcam, moments before death, iceberg, disturbing moments, everest, mountain, body

## What the format is
One real disturbing human moment on the left ~55–60%, right side fading to near-black, centred 3-line verdict in a
heavy rounded sans, white with ONE red hook word. Full spec: DESIGN_RULES.md §3 F1.

## Approved recipe (not-human v003 — seed approval, no chat corrections yet)
- Real photo (climber on the ice face), full body in frame — crampons/feet not cropped.
- Cool blue grade for snow; recover snow highlights (no blown whites); softer crunch than default; tight face treatment
  so the face reads at 168px.
- Text: "THIS IS NOT A HUMAN", NOT in red, black glow, right column x≈0.58–0.95.

## Mistakes to avoid
- Clipped snow/whites; over-crunched texture; cropping the subject's feet/limbs.
- Copying the reference's duration badge/progress bar.
