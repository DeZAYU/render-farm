3. **Same clip:** pull 3–6 frames from the source video at the timestamp
   (`yt-dlp --download-sections "*START-END" -f "worst[ext=mp4]/worst"`, `player_client=tv_simply` if blocked),

     untraceable repost that no agency holds.

   `site:newsflare.com / site:viralhog.com / site:jukinmedia.com <event>`; "<event> Storyful/ViralHog" (storyful.com

## The approved outline shape (all 6, 2026-11-13 14:05; copy this)
**How the cases were chosen**, bold-labelled paragraphs in this order:
- **Cold-open note**: topic-first, under 200 words, the range in one breath, and the milked anchors not to name.
  For a Vol 2 title, quote Vol 1's opening line and say not to reuse it (dashcam).
- **Method**: training list + which proven references the added cases came from, numbered.
- **Pool**: the reference videos/channels mined; what passed (with video IDs), and what failed and why.
- **Earlier-videos exclusion**: checked against his channel (batches/old-heritage-files/exclusions.md), naming
  near-collisions (Nehru 2021 ≠ Vol 1's 2016 Hyderabad case).
- **Ledger**: claims made, releases with reasons.
- **Freshness rule**: "proven beats new"; list the big views for the record.
- **Content filters**: what was trimmed, blurred, or skipped.
- **Fiction / hoax cases**: "none" if none.
- **Risk tiers**: every case number under GREEN / AMBER, RED drops named, and rejected angles listed as text only.
- **Per-source cap**: which reference backs which case numbers.
- **Spread**: years, countries, animals/chains, story types.
- **Order**: list order, and which similar neighbours were split.
- **Tone**: injuries stated once, uncharged people unnamed, allegations attributed.
- **Footage rule** (incl. the channel-rule waiver text), **Length** (the gate number, where the words came from),
  **Footage note for writers** (each case's strongest in/out window + mute/blur list).
- Iceberg variant: add **Most-used first** and **Visual plan and risk tiers**. Parts are named levels
  ("## Level 1 — Surface: the ones you've heard of").

**Running order:**
- Caught-on-camera columns: # | Case | Year | Clip (uploader) | Tier | Target words | Part.
- The Total row shows "N GREEN · M AMBER" and the per-part totals. Then **Links between cases**, one "A → B" line
  for each step.

**Each entry** has a `### N. <Evocative title> (<year>, <place>, <venue>) — ~N words` heading, then:
- **Status / quick facts**: date and time with conflicting sources named, people, injuries, outcome, the tier with
  the list's reason quoted, and niche use ("3 reference videos from 2 channels").
- **Source video**: each reference as "Reference only — not footage" with a ?t= timestamp.
- **Summary**: 4–7 plain paragraphs.
- **What the source video adds**: the reference's errors, corrected.
- **Extra material**: 10–15 bullets, each with [source, date].
- **Handle with care**: naming, blur, mute, and possible penalties stated as possible.
- **Footage table**: "What it shows" timed beat by beat. The Note gives uploader · subs · upload date · last channel
  upload · tier and why · how it was checked · USE IN/OUT · skip/blur/mute · waiver.
- **Sources**: news named in plain text, Commons file pages for the iceberg variant.
