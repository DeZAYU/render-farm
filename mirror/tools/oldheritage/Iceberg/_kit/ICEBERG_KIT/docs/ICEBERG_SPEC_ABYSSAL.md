# How the iceberg works in Abyssal Detective (lead's frame study, CCTV Iceberg Vol 4, 3PY5gA_hqMI). BINDING for v3.

Frames: `refs/abyssal/` (sheets made at 0:12–0:15, 0:30–0:36, and the 4.2 s after every black gap: 171, 281, 391, 870, 993, 1358, 1886, 2402 s).

## What the iceberg MEANS
The iceberg is a DEPTH METAPHOR, and the camera's position on ONE cinematic iceberg encodes the depth.
- Levels 1–2: the camera sits ABOVE the water, on the tip.
- Level 3: the shot lands AT THE WATERLINE (half above, half below).
- Levels 4+: UNDER the water, lower and darker each level, the ice body fading into murk.
Every entry is introduced ON the iceberg at its level's depth, so the viewer always knows how deep they are. It is NOT a front-on diagram with bars.

## Shots and keyframes (measured)
1. **Chart: once, in the intro (0:12–0:15, about 3 s, static).**
   - A wide, dark, desaturated photo of the berg (tip above a dark waterline) sits behind.
   - 7 thin white horizontal bars span the full width.
   - Entry names are in small typewriter type, two per row, left and right of centre. Unrevealed/deeper names are BLURRED.
   - No zooms on the chart.
2. **Level card, every level.**
   - Black 0.7–1.0 s, then a fade-up (about 0.5 s) on the WIDE berg shot: a cinematic LOW angle from the sea surface, tip above, dark body below the waterline, cloudy sky, teal grade.
   - "Level N" is in big bold typewriter (Courier-like), white, centred ON the waterline. It holds about 1.1 s.
   - Then a fast push-in (about 0.35 s) toward that level's depth. The "Level N" text scales up and flies past the camera with heavy motion blur; the berg scales up with blur. The shot lands on the close-up at the level's depth.
3. **Entry card, EVERY entry, including the first after a level card.**
   - Black 0.7 s → fade up on the iceberg CLOSE-UP at the current level's depth (tip close-up for L1–2, waterline split for L3, underwater ice for L4+).
   - A very slow drift (near-static).
   - The entry title in glowing ICE-CYAN wide caps (2 lines, centred, upper third) glitch-types in over about 0.3 s, then holds about 2.5–3 s.
   - Then a glitch/RGB-split cut straight into the case footage.
4. **Grade:** a teal/cyan-desaturated photographic look. The berg looks REAL (photographic), never cartoon/glow/diagram.
5. **Intro order:** warning card → "<channel> presents" over footage → fast footage montage → chart (brief) → Level 1 card → first entry card.

## Inside an entry (Abyssal, 0:36–2:00)
- **Footage:** almost all footage, often CCTV, with glitch/RGB hand-offs between clips.
- **Date card:** typewriter white caps on black or over footage, with the YEAR in red ("AUGUST 1, 2019"), typed in. It appears once at the entry start.
- **Name tag:** a tiny small-caps label bottom-left the first time a person appears ("CLEYTON SILVA").
- **Not used:** maps, pages and diagrams are rare. No look-here circles. No captions repeating the VO.
