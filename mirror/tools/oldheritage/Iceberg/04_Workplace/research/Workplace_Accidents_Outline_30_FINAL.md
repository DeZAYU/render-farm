### 01 · The Tank That Folded – Switz City Grain Tank

- **Opening visual:** An overhead grain tank on its steel superstructure, corn already pouring out of its side, the filmer's voice saying the frame is buckling.
- **What happened (research):** An overhead tank full of corn at White Farms failed and collapsed. As it came down it struck a power line, and the grain dust ignited in a flash fire. The account of the cause comes from the farm's own post (Jordan White) and the uploader's description. Nobody was hurt; White reported "no one was hurt" and the uploader wrote "There were no injuries!" The video spread through farm-safety sites, which used it as an example of grain-dust hazards.

### 05 · Nine Days at Alemoa – Ultracargo, Santos
