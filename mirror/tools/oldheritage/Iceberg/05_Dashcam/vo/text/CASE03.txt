This camera sits high in the cab of a truck, a little after ten at night on September 6, 2016. It's on the raised road that carries freight into Calais, in northern France, the last stretch before the ferries to Britain. For a few seconds there's nothing on screen but an empty curve and a concrete barrier.

That year, thousands of people were living in a camp outside town known as the Jungle, and many were trying to reach Britain hidden in the back of trucks. Branches dragged into the lanes were a known way to make a driver stop long enough to climb aboard. The day before this was filmed, truckers had joined farmers in blocking the highway to demand the camp be shut.

The thing to watch is the top of that concrete barrier, and the dark tangle lying across both lanes.

Online, this clip usually gets told with another story attached, about rocks and concrete through a windshield and a driver who was badly cut. That's a different attack on a different truck, months earlier. In this one, nobody's seen throwing anything at the cab, and the glass doesn't break.

So who was behind the wheel, and did anyone get into the trailer? Nobody's ever reported either. Seven weeks later the Jungle was cleared.
