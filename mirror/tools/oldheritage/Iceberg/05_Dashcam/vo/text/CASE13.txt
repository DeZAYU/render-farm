A YouTuber who goes by Bear With Hat was leaving a friend's house in Keller, Texas, one night in June 2016 when he noticed a white car close behind him. He thought it was a Dodge Challenger.

So he tested it. He signaled for the Interstate 35W on-ramp and cut onto the service road at the last second, but at the next overpass the white car was already waiting at the light. He made a U-turn and pulled over, and it pulled over right behind him.

When he stopped in a turning lane outside a hospital, the white car stopped right in front of him. He didn't have a dashcam, so he held up his phone, hoping to catch a plate for a 911 call. Watch the passenger side of the white car.

He said later that he swung out so close he'd have hit the passenger, the man standing beside the car, if he hadn't jumped clear. The phone fell to the floor, and that's why the picture goes dark.
