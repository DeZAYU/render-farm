Aswan Yap was driving through Bukit Tunku with a friend one Friday night in May 2017, somewhere between Lake Gardens and Kenny Hills. It's a quiet hill district in the middle of Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, where the road winds through patches of jungle. Local stories call it one of the city's most haunted streets, though none of them has ever been backed by a record.

They round a bend marked with yellow chevrons, and Yap stops the car. The shape is tiny at this distance, so look at the center line far up the road, where the headlights fade out.

Yap later said he was convinced it was a ghost, and that his car's log showed a fault at the moment he saw the figure. Others thought it'd been someone waiting for a car to stop so they could rob the driver. No police report was ever made public, and some people online have doubted the video is real at all.

That fault isn't as spooky as it sounds, though. The warning on his dashboard was the traction control, and his friend offered a plain reason on the spot, wondering out loud if they'd reversed too fast. I like that the clip hands us the ordinary answer before anyone online can dress it up.
